INTERNSHIP REPORT (KERJA PRAKTEK) · 2025

Risk Analysis of Lightweight Brick Installation Using Scaffolding with the Job Safety Analysis (JSA) Method on a Hospital Construction Project

Occupational safety study carried out during my HSE internship at the RSPON Cawang hospital project (PT WIKA–PP KSO). Nine work activities and 18 hazards were assessed with JSA using a likelihood × severity matrix: seven activities rated high risk and two medium, with scaffolding inspection and assembly scoring highest. Risk controls follow the hierarchy of controls, from routine scaffold inspection and safe work procedures to engineering measures such as guardrails, toe boards, and safety nets.

Site: RSPON Cawang Hospital Project, East Jakarta Academic Supervisor: Ir. Agustian Suseno, S.T., M.M. Keywords: JSA · Scaffolding · Risk Assessment · Construction Safety Language: Indonesian
Read Report (PDF) Download
Key results
  • Assessed 9 work activities and 18 hazards on a hospital construction project using Job Safety Analysis (JSA).
  • Identified scaffolding inspection and assembly as the highest-risk activities.
  • Recommended controls based on the hierarchy of controls, from safe work procedures to guardrails, toe boards, and safety nets.
